What Is Advanced Amino Formula?

Advanced Amino Formula is a dietary supplement developed by Advanced Bionutritionals. It combines eight essential amino acids that the human body needs to build proteins.

Essential amino acids cannot be produced in sufficient amounts by the body, which means they must come from food or appropriate supplementation.

The formula comes in tablet form, making it a convenient option for people who want to complement their daily nutritional routine without preparing protein shakes.

How Does It Compare to BCAA Supplements?

BCAA supplements typically contain three branched-chain amino acids: leucine, isoleucine and valine.

Advanced Amino Formula includes those three plus five additional essential amino acids in the same formula.
